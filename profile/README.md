# 🌌 Welcome to viewgalaxies! 🚀

Discover the cosmos, one galaxy at a time. **viewgalaxies** is an interactive space exploration platform designed to bring the wonders of the universe right to your screen. Whether you are an aspiring astronomer, a student, or just a curious mind, we provide the ultimate portal to expand your space knowledge.

---

## 🌟 What We Offer

- **🔭 Interactive Star Maps:** Explore constellations, planets, and distant galaxies in real-time.
- **📚 Cosmic Encyclopedia:** Comprehensive, easy-to-understand articles about black holes, exoplanets, dark matter, and space history.
- **🚀 Daily Space News:** Keep up with the latest discoveries from NASA, ESA, and global space agencies.
- **💫 Quiz & Missions:** Test your space knowledge and unlock virtual badges as you learn.

---

## 🛠️ Our Tech Stack

We build the future of space education using modern technologies:
- **Frontend:** React.js / Next.js, Tailwind CSS
- **3D Visualization:** Three.js / WebGL
- **Data & APIs:** NASA Open APIs, SpaceX API
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express, GraphQL
